Consumption is by far one of the biggest factors that affect how we carry ourselves throughout our lives. What we choose to consume would naturally choose the rest of our decisions. It will carry into how we react, how we think, how we move, and the choices we make. 

Consumption is a humans fuel. We take things in that will support how we go about life. Fuel is often use to run a motor ; for us that's our brains, body, and muscles. We all take things in every day. We consume often through a mixture of things like taking in information, putting food into our bodies, listening to music that is subconsciously feeding into our mindset, and the list goes on and on. When we hear something, we watch something, we eat something, it affects us; for better or for worse.

As an individual we have to protect what we consume and we have to make sure we consume intentionally. It's important to protect your peace and block out the noise. We have to be able to identify what fuel It's going heal us and push us forward versus what fuel is going to slow our motor.

---
### Check the creditability of the source when consuming

One of the most common ways we consume information is word of mouth; from people within our inner circle. And this doesn't necessarily have to be the people we invite into our lives this can be people we are constantly around because of the circumstances. Like neighbors, classmates, or coworkers. 

When we consume information that comes out of other people's mouth it is not always the best source. The majority of people don't fact check, don't research, and don't know what they're talking about. 

They also don't take the time to prioritize and work on their selves. And that is important. When someone doesn't prioritize their own well being, especially their mindset and mental well being, they often see the glass half empty. Which means naturally their mind goes to the worst case scenarios.

This is not me at all trying to putting the majority of our population down. This is for me to better understand how important it is to know the credibility of the source. 

And when we find ourselves playing a thought or idea in our head we have to first figure out what source we got it from and is that source credible.

---
*Checking the "credibility" of the source when consuming goes beyond on just face to face conversations. It can also be: Checking the ingredients before you eat something, looking up the lyrics and meaning behind an album before you constantly play the message in your ear, and the most common, doing your own research when  consuming media.
