### What is a digital garden?

**The Short Answer**: It's someone's collection of interconnected notes that evolve over time. 

What makes digital gardening special is the internal linking system that allows notes to connect and lead back to each other. 

>  *Such interconnection creates a trail of ideas that readers can follow. - [Anne-Laure Le Cunff](https://nesslabs.com/digital-garden-set-up)*

Don't think of the writing as post or articles; think notes, rough drafts even. It's purpose is not to be a polished essay for the public to read; although some "gardens" are made public, the purpose is for the creator of the notes. It's their way to cultivate their ideas, see how they may connect, and see where one idea can lead to the next.

And that's the thing with digital gardening, as you write, you find phrases within your writing that you want to later dive deeper into, which can do wonders for exploring one topic in depth.
