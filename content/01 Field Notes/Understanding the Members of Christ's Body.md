first are [[Understanding the Members of Christ's Body#Apostles|apostles]],

---

In 1 Corinthians 12, Paul writes about how we, as Christians, should function together as one body with many parts. The verses leading up to verse 28 emphasize the importance of every member of the church and the role each of us plays in the body of Christ. He writes:

> _26_ | If one part suffers, all the parts suffer with it, and if one part is honored, all the parts are glad.
> 
> _27_ | All of you together are Christ’s body, and each of you is a part of it.

Once we give our lives to Christ and become one with the Spirit, we are no longer alone. The same is true in our service. We do not serve independently of one another, but alongside God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit, as well as the other members of Christ’s body who have been called to serve.

God has given each of us particular gifts, not to be taken advantage of for our own selfish desires, but to be used in service to others. When we dedicate our time to using the gifts to help those around us, and do so together as one body, we demonstrate what true [[Unity in the Body]] of Christ looks like.

### Defining The Roles of The Church
#### Apostles

>The word _apostle_ means “one who is sent out.” In the New Testament, there are two primary usages of the word _apostle_. The first is in specifically referring to the [twelve apostles](https://www.gotquestions.org/twelve-apostles-disciples-12.html) of Jesus Christ. The second is in generically referring to other individuals who are sent out to be messengers/ambassadors of Jesus Christ. 
>

[gotquestions.org](https://www.gotquestions.org/what-is-an-apostle.html)

*Paul listed apostles first because they carry the most authority of any gift.*

---
#### Prophets

> In a general sense, a prophet is a person who speaks God’s truth to others. The English word _prophet_ comes from the Greek word _prophetes_, which can mean “one who speaks forth” or “advocate.”

> A true prophet of God will be committed to speaking God’s truth. He or she will never contradict God’s revealed Word.

[gotquestions.org](https://www.gotquestions.org/prophet-Bible.html)

*Keep in mind not everyone who says they speak God's word is a prophet, the Bible warns us about false prophets.*

---
#### Teachers 

> It is a gift given by the Holy Spirit, enabling one to effectively communicate the truths of the Bible to others. It is most often, but not always, used in the context of the local church.

> The gifted teacher is one who has the unique ability to clearly instruct and communicate knowledge, specifically the doctrines of the faith and truths of the Bible.

> There are several contexts in which the gift of teaching can be used: Sunday school classes, Bible schools, colleges, seminaries, and home Bible studies. The one with the gift can teach either individuals or groups

> No new material originates from one with the gift of teaching. The teacher simply explains or expounds the meaning of the Bible’s text.

> Teaching is a supernatural gift of the Holy Spirit. One without this gift can understand the Bible as he hears or reads it, but he cannot explain it as one with the gift can.

[gotquestions.org](https://www.gotquestions.org/gift-of-teaching.html)

*If a teacher falls so can many people with them, it's like blind leading the blind. That's why those who teach are judged more strictly.*

#### Those who do Miracles

> Miracles are supernatural events that occur outside the bounds of what is natural. By definition, miracles are rare and out of the ordinary.

> Those who have the gift of miracles have the ability, by the [Holy Spirit](https://newspring.cc/articles/who-is-the-holy-spirit), to do powerful and miraculous things, such as raising the dead or restoring the blind to sight, speech to the mute, hearing to the deaf, and the use of limbs to the lame.

> The gift of miracles initiates, restores, and strengthens faith in God.

[newspring.cc](https://newspring.cc/articles/how-to-know-the-gift-of-miracles-when-you-see-it)

#### The gift of Healing

> The spiritual gift of healing is the supernatural manifestation of the Spirit of God that miraculously brings healing and deliverance from disease and/or infirmity. It is the power of God that destroys the work of sin and/or the devil in the human body, such as the healings that Jesus and the disciples performed ([Matthew 4:24](https://www.bibleref.com/Matthew/4/Matthew-4-24.html); [15:30](https://www.bibleref.com/Matthew/15/Matthew-15-30.html); [Acts 5:15-16](https://www.bibleref.com/Acts/5/Acts-5-15.html); [28:8-9](https://www.bibleref.com/Acts/28/Acts-28-8.html)).

[gotquestions.org](https://www.gotquestions.org/gift-of-healing.html)

#### Who can help Others

> Helping in the body of Christ can take a variety of forms. Some see the gift of helps as given to those who are willing to “lend a hand” and do even the most mundane and disagreeable tasks with a spirit of humility and grace. Helpers are often those who volunteer to work regularly around church buildings and grounds, often laboring in obscurity. Others see helping as assisting the widows and elderly or families to accomplish daily tasks, coming alongside to render assistance in those areas where help is needed. These helpers render a gift of service in the broadest sense, assisting and supporting the body of Christ.

>  Those with the spiritual gift of helps have been given the unique ability to identify those who are struggling with doubt, fears, and other spiritual battles. They move toward those in spiritual need with a kind word, an understanding and compassionate demeanor, and the unique ability to speak scriptural truth in convicting and loving fashion.

[gotquestions.org](https://www.gotquestions.org/gift-of-helps.html)

#### The gift of Leadership

> ...one who is set over others or who presides or rules or who attends with [diligence](https://www.gotquestions.org/Bible-diligence.html) and care to a thing.

> They understand that they are not absolute rulers but are themselves subject to the One who is over them all, the Lord Jesus who is the Head of the church. Recognizing his place in the hierarchy of the administration of the body of Christ prevents the gifted leader from succumbing to pride or a sense of entitlement. The truly gifted Christian leader recognizes that he is but a slave of Christ and a servant of those he leads.

> As shepherds of God’s people, gifted leaders rule with diligence and possess the ability to discern true spiritual needs from “felt” needs. They lead others to maturity in the faith. The Christian leader leads others to grow in their ability to discern for themselves that which comes from God versus that which is cultural or temporary.

> The spiritual gift of leadership is given by God to men and women who will help the church to grow and thrive beyond the current generation.

[gotquestions.org](https://www.gotquestions.org/gift-of-leadership.html)

### Identifying Spiritual Gifts 
When we focus too much on trying to identify the gifts God has given us, we often miss the opportunities right in front of us to serve. God already has a plan for us, and He will equip us with the gifts necessary to fulfill it. Rather than waiting to discover what those gifts are, we must learn to trust Him and allow Him to deliver us into opportunities where He can use us in miraculous ways. Sometimes, that simply means taking the first step toward serving God's people without knowing which gifts He has instilled in us. When we put our trust in God and are willing to follow where He leads, He will guide us without fail.
