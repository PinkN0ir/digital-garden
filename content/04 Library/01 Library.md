Media, books, sources; they all play a big role in what we believe in. That's why [[consumption should be done intentionally]]; it's important to protect what knowledge we consume. 

When selecting what resources I'm going to consume I look for a topic I want to become more knowledgeable in. I'm dedicated to being a [[student of life]] so when I consume media I look for what insight I can take from it. And there is a lesson that can be learned from every story, nonfiction and fiction.

But learning never stops at consuming. When we just consume we can easily fall into the traps of [[overconsumption]]. Which never benefits us if we're not retaining the knowledge we are taking in. So I like to consume less and process, apply, and retain more. 

**What that can look like when played out is**:

| Consuming                                 | Processing                               | Application                                              | Retention                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Receiving information on a specific topic | Understanding the information            | Applying the knowledge learned                           | Ensuring that the information will be remembered                                     |
| *Reading a book*                          | *Annotating, common-placing, reflecting* | *Completing a project based on the information consumed* | *Taking what you learned throughout the learning process and teaching it to someone* |
